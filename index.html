YS: something simple! something you can make in a sitting or two, and have a lot of fun doing

WS: something else simple and small! a xiao board, stickers, a zine, something small! the more bite-sized projects you make, the more you get!

FAQ:

what can i make?

really anything! it just needs to be simple! you could make a small python script, a simple website, a little extension.

feel free to ask in the slack channel (#simpler) if you aren't sure!

am i allowed to join?

if you are 13-18 (inclusive), then yes!

i'm a beginner! can i still participate?

yes! you don't need to make something complicated for this! all you need to do is make something small that you're interested in making!

what is Hack Club?

Hack Club is the nonprofit running this event! Hack Club is built by teens for teens and runs tons of "YSWS" programs like these! You can learn more at hackclub.com

and project requirments

For every project you ship, you get one credit! Even though projects must be simple, in order to submit, your project needs to do something ("something" could just be displaying information or being fun!)
